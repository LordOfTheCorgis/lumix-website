## California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

This Notice supplements our Privacy Policy and applies to California residents. It explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Where this Notice and our Privacy Policy differ, this Notice governs for California residents. Everything else in the Privacy Policy still applies.

We act as a service provider in many cases when processing data on behalf of customers using our hosting infrastructure. When you rent a server from us and your own users' data ends up on it, you are the business and we are the service provider. We process that data on your instructions and for no purpose of our own.

## Categories of Personal Information Collected

In the past 12 months, we have collected the following categories:

### Identifiers

Name, email, IP address, account ID, device identifiers

### Customer Records

Billing info, payment details, contact information

### Commercial Information

Service usage, purchases, subscription history

### Internet Activity

Logs, browsing behavior, interactions with services

### Geolocation Data

IP-based location for fraud prevention and security. This is approximate, derived from IP address, and is not precise location data.

### Professional Data

Company name or role (if provided)

### Inferences

Service preferences derived from usage patterns

## Sensitive Personal Information

We may process limited sensitive personal information such as authentication credentials and payment data. This information is used only to operate services, process payments, and prevent fraud.

Because we use it only for those purposes, it falls within the exemptions the CPRA provides, and the right to limit its use described below has limited practical effect.

We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about users.

## Sources of Information

- Directly from you (account creation, purchases, support)

- Automatically via logs, cookies, and analytics

- Third parties (payment processors, fraud prevention tools)

## Purpose of Collection

- Service delivery and hosting operations

- Security, fraud prevention, and abuse mitigation

- Billing and account management

- Service improvement and diagnostics

- Legal compliance

We do not use personal information for purposes materially different from those listed without telling you first.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA. "Share" has a specific meaning in these laws, covering disclosure for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not do that either.

We disclose personal information for business purposes to:

- Service providers (hosting, payments, support tools)

- Security and fraud prevention partners

- Legal authorities when required by law

Each service provider is bound by contract to use the information only to perform the service we engaged it for.

## Financial Incentives

We do not offer financial incentives, discounts, or pricing differences in exchange for personal information.

## Your California Rights

- **Right to know** what data is collected, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to

- **Right to delete** personal information we hold about you

- **Right to correct** inaccurate data

- **Right to opt out** of sale/sharing (not applicable, because we do neither)

- **Right to limit** use of sensitive data

- **Right to non-discrimination** for exercising any right above

Exercising these rights will never change the price you pay or the quality of service you receive.

## Exercising Your Rights

You can submit requests via:

Email: contact@lumixsolutions.org

We will verify your identity before processing requests and respond within 45 days. If a request is complex and we need more time, we will tell you within that window and may extend it as the CCPA permits.

If we decline a request, we will explain why.

## Verification

We may require identity verification before fulfilling requests, including account confirmation or additional proof. The proof we ask for is proportionate to the sensitivity of what is being requested, and we use it only to verify you.

Authorized agents must provide written permission or legal authorization.

## Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for operational, legal, and security purposes. These periods match the ones in our Privacy Policy.

- **Accounts:** active + 30 days

- **Billing:** 7 years

- **Logs:** 90 days

- **Support:** 2 years

## Shine the Light Law

We do not share personal information with third parties for direct marketing purposes. California residents may request this disclosure once per year under Civil Code section 1798.83, and our answer will be the same.
