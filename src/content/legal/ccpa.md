This California Privacy Notice (“Notice”) supplements the Lumix Solutions Privacy Policy and provides additional information for California residents about our collection, use, disclosure, and retention of personal information. It also explains the rights available under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations (collectively, the “CCPA”).

In this Notice, “Lumix Solutions,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Lumix Solutions. “You” and “your” refer to California residents whose personal information is covered by this Notice.

Please read this Notice together with our Privacy Policy. If the two documents conflict concerning the processing of personal information covered by the CCPA, this Notice controls for California residents. The remaining provisions of our Privacy Policy continue to apply.

## 1. Scope and Our Role

This Notice describes our practices in connection with our website, customer accounts, hosting services, billing, support, and related business activities. “Personal information” generally means information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular consumer or household. Information excluded from the CCPA’s definition, such as qualifying publicly available, deidentified, or aggregate consumer information, is not personal information for purposes of this Notice.

Our role depends on the information involved and the circumstances in which we process it. For example, we determine how customer account and billing information is used to administer our relationship with customers. We also process information on behalf of customers who use our hosting infrastructure.

Where we process personal information as a service provider or contractor for a customer under the CCPA, we do so under the applicable agreement, the customer’s instructions, and the restrictions imposed by law. We do not use that information for unrelated purposes outside those permitted by our agreement and applicable law. Renting hosting infrastructure does not, by itself, determine whether a customer qualifies as a “business” under the CCPA.

If your information is stored in a website, application, or server operated by one of our customers, that customer generally determines how the information is collected and used. You should direct privacy requests concerning that information to the relevant customer. If we receive a request concerning information we process on a customer’s behalf, we will address it consistently with our contractual and legal obligations, including assisting the customer where required.

## 3. Sensitive Personal Information

Some information used to authenticate an account or complete a payment may qualify as sensitive personal information under the CCPA. This can include an account login or financial account, debit card, or credit card number combined with a password, security code, or other credentials that permit access to the account. Not every item of billing or payment information qualifies as sensitive personal information.

We use and disclose the limited sensitive personal information we process only as reasonably necessary and proportionate to provide the services you request, administer and secure your account, process payments, and prevent, detect, or investigate fraud and security incidents, within the purposes permitted by the CCPA and its regulations.

We do not process sensitive personal information for the purpose of inferring characteristics about you.

Because our uses and disclosures are limited in this way, we do not currently engage in processing that requires us to offer a right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information. This does not exempt the information from other applicable privacy obligations or remove other rights you may have concerning it.

## 4. Sources of Personal Information

We collect personal information from the following categories of sources:

- **Directly from you.** Information you provide when creating or maintaining an account, purchasing a service, submitting billing details, or communicating with support.
- **Automatically through your interactions with us.** Information generated through website and service activity, including logs, cookies, and analytics technologies.
- **Payment processors and fraud prevention providers.** Information associated with processing transactions, assessing potential fraud, and protecting accounts and services.

The sources involved vary according to the information and the service being provided.

## 5. Why We Collect and Use Personal Information

We collect and use personal information for the following business purposes:

- **Providing and operating services.** Establishing accounts, provisioning hosting resources, delivering requested services, and maintaining the operation and availability of our infrastructure.
- **Billing and account administration.** Processing payments, managing subscriptions, maintaining transaction records, and addressing billing inquiries.
- **Customer support.** Responding to questions, investigating reported problems, and providing assistance with accounts and services.
- **Security, fraud prevention, and abuse mitigation.** Authenticating users, investigating suspicious activity, addressing misuse of our services, and protecting customers and infrastructure.
- **Diagnostics and service improvement.** Identifying technical issues, understanding service usage, and improving reliability, functionality, and performance.
- **Legal compliance.** Meeting applicable legal obligations, responding to legally required requests, and maintaining records required by law.

Our collection, use, retention, and disclosure of personal information will be reasonably necessary and proportionate to the purposes for which the information was collected or another disclosed purpose compatible with the context of collection.

Before collecting additional categories of personal information or using information for additional purposes that are incompatible with the disclosed purposes, we will provide any additional notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law.

### No sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising

We do not sell personal information or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we have not done so during the preceding 12 months.

“Sale” can include a disclosure in exchange for monetary or other valuable consideration. “Sharing” has a specific meaning under the CCPA and generally concerns disclosure for advertising that targets a consumer based on activity across distinct businesses or services.

Our statement that we do not “share” personal information in this legal sense does not mean that we never disclose information. The business-purpose disclosures described above are distinct from sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Our no-sale and no-sharing practices apply to all consumers, including consumers under 16 years of age.

## 7. Your California Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable exceptions and verification requirements, California residents have the following rights concerning personal information covered by the CCPA.

### Right to know and access

You may request that we disclose:

- The categories of personal information we collected about you.
- The categories of sources from which we collected that information.
- The business or commercial purposes for collecting, selling, or sharing the information, as applicable.
- The categories of third parties to whom we disclosed personal information.
- The categories of information sold or shared, if any, and the categories of third parties receiving each category.
- The categories of information disclosed for a business purpose and the categories of recipients receiving each category.
- The specific pieces of personal information we collected about you.

Where required, we will provide your information in a portable and readily usable format that allows you to transmit it to another entity without hindrance.

Requests are not necessarily limited to information collected during the preceding 12 months. You may also request older information collected on or after January 1, 2022, subject to applicable legal limitations. We are not required to retain information solely to fulfill a possible future access request.

### Right to delete

You may request deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to exceptions permitted by law.

For example, we may retain information where an applicable exception permits us to complete a transaction, provide a service you requested, protect security and integrity, or comply with a legal obligation. We will assess the circumstances of your request rather than treating these examples as an automatic reason to refuse deletion.

Where required, we will also direct our service providers and contractors, and notify other relevant recipients, to delete the information.

### Right to correct

You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you. We will consider the nature of the information, the purposes for which it is processed, and relevant supporting information when evaluating your request, and will use commercially reasonable efforts to correct inaccuracies as required by law.

### Right to opt out of sale or sharing

You have the right to direct a business to stop selling your personal information or sharing it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Because Lumix Solutions does neither, you do not need to submit an opt-out request to prevent those activities under our current practices. A browser-based preference signal, such as Global Privacy Control, communicates an opt-out preference; our current no-sale and no-sharing practices apply whether or not you enable that signal.

### Right to limit certain uses of sensitive personal information

You have the right to limit certain uses and disclosures of sensitive personal information when a business processes that information beyond the purposes permitted without offering this right.

As explained above, our current processing is limited to those permitted purposes. You may contact us with questions about the sensitive personal information we process or to exercise another applicable privacy right concerning that information.

### Right to non-discrimination

We will not unlawfully discriminate or retaliate against you for exercising your CCPA rights. This includes denying services, charging different prices, providing a different quality of service, or suggesting that you will receive different treatment because you exercised a right, except as permitted by law.

If you ask us to delete information necessary to operate your account or deliver a requested service, fulfilling that request may mean that we can no longer provide the affected functionality. Where applicable, we will explain that consequence so you can make an informed choice.

## 9. Identity Verification and Authorized Agents

Before fulfilling a request to know, delete, or correct personal information, we will take reasonable steps to verify that the requester is the person to whom the information relates or is authorized to act for that person.

Verification may involve confirming control of an existing account or matching information you provide against information we already maintain. The information requested and the level of verification will be proportionate to the sensitivity of the information and the risk of harm from an unauthorized disclosure, deletion, or correction.

We use information collected specifically for verification only for verification, security, or fraud prevention, as permitted by law. We will not require unnecessary personal information as a condition of exercising your rights.

You may designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. We may ask the agent for proof of your signed permission and, where permitted, ask you to verify your identity directly with us or confirm that you authorized the request. Different requirements may apply when an agent acts under a valid power of attorney recognized by California law.

If we cannot reasonably verify a request, we will explain that limitation and will not disclose, delete, or alter information in a way that could compromise another person’s privacy or security.

## 10. Response Timing and Request Handling

We will acknowledge receipt of a request to know, delete, or correct personal information within **10 business days** and provide information about how we will process it.

We will provide a substantive response within **45 calendar days** of receiving the request. This period begins when we receive the request, rather than when verification is complete.

Where reasonably necessary and permitted by law, we may extend the response period by up to an additional **45 calendar days**. If an extension is needed, we will notify you within the initial response period and explain the reason.

We generally respond to requests without charge. If a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or decline to act only to the extent permitted by law, and we will explain the basis for that decision.

If we deny all or part of a request, we will explain the applicable reason unless prohibited from doing so by law. Where possible and required, we will fulfill the portion that is not subject to an exception.

## 13. California “Shine the Light” Law

California Civil Code section 1798.83, commonly known as the “Shine the Light” law, provides qualifying California residents with rights concerning certain disclosures of personal information to third parties for those third parties’ direct marketing purposes.

We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes. You may contact us at the email address above with questions about this practice or to make an applicable request under that law.

## 14. Changes to This Notice

We will review this Notice at least once every 12 months and update it as necessary to reflect our practices and applicable legal requirements. The “Last updated” date identifies the most recent revision.

Where a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide that notice or obtain that consent before implementing the affected processing.

## 15. Confirmation of California Residency

The California-specific rights described in this Notice apply to California residents as defined by applicable law. When you submit a request to know, access, delete, or correct personal information under the CCPA, **we will generally ask you to confirm that you are a California resident** as part of determining whether those rights apply to your request.

We will first consider your confirmation and relevant information already available to us. If additional information is reasonably necessary to establish your eligibility, we may request limited supporting information and explain what is needed. Any request for supporting information will be proportionate to that purpose, and you may redact information that is not necessary for the residency check. Please do not send identification documents or other sensitive records unless we specifically request them through an appropriate submission method.

California residency does not necessarily depend on your physical location when you submit a request. We will not treat an IP address or a temporary absence from California as conclusive evidence that you are ineligible.

We will use information collected for this check only to assess eligibility, protect against fraud, and document compliance as permitted by law. Residency checks do not extend the response deadlines in Section 10 and will not be used to impose identity-verification requirements on requests to opt out of sale or sharing or to limit sensitive-information use. This section does not affect any privacy rights you may have under other applicable laws.
