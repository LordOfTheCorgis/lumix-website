## 1. Introduction

Lumix Solutions ("we," "our," or "us") provides web hosting and related infrastructure services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

This policy covers the information we handle as a business: the account you create with us, the payments you make, the support conversations you have with us, and the technical records our infrastructure produces while running your services. It applies to our website, our billing portal, and our control panel.

It does not cover the data you choose to store on servers you rent from us. That data is yours, you decide what goes on it, and section 13 explains the difference in more detail.

By using our services, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Personal Information

Collected when you create an account, place an order, or contact us for support.

- Name, email, phone number

- Billing details (address, payment info)

- Account credentials

- Support communications

### Automatically Collected

Recorded by our systems as you use the website, the billing portal, and the control panel.

- IP address and approximate location

- Device and browser data

- Usage activity and timestamps

- Referring/exit pages

### Service Data

Produced by the infrastructure itself while your services run. This is operational telemetry about the machines, not an inspection of the content you host on them.

- Server logs and diagnostics

- Traffic patterns (for DDoS mitigation)

- Resource usage

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information above only for the purposes below, and we do not repurpose it for anything unrelated to running and protecting the service.

- **Provide and maintain services.** Create and administer your account, provision what you order, and keep it running.

- **Process billing.** Take payment, issue invoices, handle renewals, refunds, and disputes.

- **Respond to support requests.** Investigate what you report, which sometimes means reviewing logs and diagnostics for the affected service.

- **Prevent abuse, fraud, and attacks.** Detect and mitigate DDoS activity, identify fraudulent orders, and enforce our Terms of Service.

- **Improve performance and reliability.** Understand capacity, latency, and failure patterns so we can plan and fix.

- **Comply with legal obligations.** Meet tax, accounting, and lawful-process requirements.

## 4. Information Sharing

We share personal information only in the situations below, and only to the extent needed for that situation.

- **Service providers.** Vendors that process payments, supply infrastructure, or operate our support tooling. Each receives only what it needs to perform its function and is bound by agreement to handle it accordingly.

- **Legal authorities.** When required by law, subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process, and when necessary to establish or defend legal claims.

- **Business transfers.** If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction. This policy continues to apply to it unless and until you are told otherwise.

We do not sell personal data. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it with third parties for their own advertising.

## 5. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data we hold.

- **Encryption (TLS/SSL).** Traffic between you and our services is encrypted in transit.

- **Access controls.** Access to systems and customer data is limited to staff whose role requires it.

- **Monitoring and intrusion detection.** Systems are monitored for unauthorized access and abnormal activity.

- **Security audits.** Configurations and controls are reviewed on an ongoing basis.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In case of a breach, we will notify affected users as required by law.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as it serves the purpose it was collected for, then delete or anonymize it.

- **Accounts:** active + 30 days

- **Billing:** up to 7 years

- **Logs:** up to 90 days

- **Support tickets:** 2 years

Billing records are held longest because tax and accounting rules require it. Logs are held shortest because their value is operational and short-lived.

Data may be deleted or anonymized upon request unless legally required. Where we are required to retain something, we retain only that record and only for as long as the requirement lasts.

## 8. Cookies

We use cookies for functionality, analytics, and preferences.

- **Session cookies.** Keep you signed in and maintain your session. Deleted when you close the browser.

- **Persistent cookies.** Remember preferences between visits, so you do not have to set them again.

- **Analytics tools.** Measure aggregate usage so we can understand which pages and features get used. We use PostHog for this, and it is the only analytics provider we operate on our website.

- **Embedded content.** Our home page embeds a reviews widget served by Trustpilot. It is requested only if you scroll far enough to reach it, and once it loads Trustpilot sets its own cookies inside it, including ones it uses to measure how often the widget is seen. We cannot read those cookies and they are governed by Trustpilot's own cookie and privacy policies, not this one.

Our own analytics cookies are not set unless you accept them. Until you do, no PostHog code is loaded at all, and declining means none is ever loaded. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser, which brings the choice back. The Trustpilot widget is separate from that choice: it is content rather than analytics, and it loads for everyone who scrolls to it.

We honour Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we record an opt-out automatically and never ask you.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking functional cookies will prevent parts of the billing portal and control panel from working.

## 13. Customer Data

Customers are responsible for data they host. We act as a processor.

This is the most important distinction in this policy. You decide what data goes onto the servers you rent, who can reach it, and how long it stays. We provide and secure the infrastructure underneath it. We do not review, mine, or use the content you host, and we access it only when you ask us to for support, or when we are legally compelled to.

If your own users have privacy rights in that data, those requests are yours to answer, not ours. We will assist where we reasonably can.

## 14. Legal Basis

Where the law requires us to identify a basis for processing, we rely on the following.

- **Contract.** Processing needed to deliver the service you ordered.

- **Legal obligation.** Processing required by tax, accounting, or law enforcement rules.

- **Legitimate interest.** Processing needed to secure the network, prevent abuse and fraud, and keep the service reliable, weighed against your rights.

- **Consent.** Where we ask for it, such as optional communications. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
